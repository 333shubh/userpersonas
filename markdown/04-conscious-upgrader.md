## 1. Persona name and archetype

**The Conscious Upgrader**, embodied by **Sprig**, a calm noodle sprout or bowl character with leaves, grains and balanced proportions. The archetype is the planner who likes comfort food but wants every meal to feel like a considered choice.

## 2. Short persona summary

Sprig still wants the taste of Maggi; what changes is how it is made. Sprig plans ahead, picks the variant that fits a personal routine, uses half the sachet and **adds**: greens, an egg, lemon, seeds. The question at the shelf is "does this fit my goals?", and the pleasure is a bowl that feels both familiar and deliberate. Sprig is never punishing itself: the upgrade is the fun part.

## 4. Day-in-the-life narrative

07:30, cool light through the kitchen window. Sprig planned this breakfast-lunch last night: a pack of Veg Atta noodles, a handful of spinach, a soft egg, a squeeze of lemon. Half the tastemaker in, the rest folded back for another day. The bowl is styled almost without thinking: greens on one side, egg in the middle, seeds on top. Eaten slowly at the table before the first call. The pan is washed straight away; it is part of the routine.

## 6. Why they choose Maggi

Maggi passes Sprig's gate, "does this fit my goals?", when it works as a base Sprig can build on, with the familiar taste kept. Sprig values transparency: transparency is very important to 65% of consumers for sustainable food choices (evidence, Medium). Sprig is also aware that critics have questioned the sodium content of some variants in older label analyses (evidence, Medium; formulations may have changed). That is exactly why Sprig's story stays additive and why any communication near health needs Nestlé review.

## 12. Visual-world design system

Sprig's world is printed with very little ink on uncoated paper: fills cover at most 60% of any illustration, every colour is muted (HSV saturation at most 0.75), and the paper fibre shows through. Shapes are leaf ovals and grain seeds, and everything sits level. The **"what's in your bowl"** label component lists what Sprig added, which supports transparency without making claims.

**Accessibility notes.** Every text pair passes WCAG AA. The leaf green is used only for icons and graphics (3:1 or more), never body text. The greens-versus-spice distinction in the add-in strip is carried by pictogram shape as well as colour, because leaf and terracotta sit closest under tritanopia simulation.

## 17. Risks, tensions and objections

- **Punishment framing.** Sprig must never feel like a diet version of Maggi; the brief forbids making the health persona feel like a punishment.
- **Implied claims.** Holding an atta pack sits close to health positioning. No claim text, no "better for you" language; needs Nestlé review.
- **Label scrutiny.** Older label analyses raised sodium questions (evidence, Medium); current labels must be re-checked before anything is said.
- **Overlap.** Sprig shares "planned" behaviour with Stack, but Sprig plans for personal fit, Stack for household value.

Sprig moves with restraint: no squash, no overshoot, a balanced 4-second idle where the leaves sway a beat behind the body. Reaction loop: a calm, satisfied nod. Micro-animation: greens drifting down into the bowl. Consumption scene (8 s): morning light, pack opened, half sachet, greens added, first unhurried bite.

**In 3D.** The idle, reaction and scene loops are built from rendered layers of the figure (head, body, arms, props, steam) and from an 8-view turntable, keeping these exact timings. Nothing is re-modelled for motion.

## 25. Custom "2" glyph, wordmark and numerals

Sprig's "2" grows as a stem with a leaf at the hook (35° from vertical), with gentle organic stress in the stroke. Its strokes stay above the 12.5% minimum, and the counter is generous, so it reads at 16 px. Numerals are a light serif with the same organic stress.
