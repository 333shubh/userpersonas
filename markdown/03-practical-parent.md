## 1. Persona name and archetype

**The Practical Parent**, embodied by **Nest**, a warm noodle-nest kitchen helper wearing an apron. The archetype is the parent or carer whose real problem is one meal, right now, that the child will actually eat.

## 2. Short persona summary

Nest buys Maggi for someone else's plate. The decision is made by the child's yes, not the parent's appetite. It is the dependable after-school answer when time is short and patience is shorter, and its quiet reward is two minutes of calm in a busy afternoon ("gets two quiet minutes"). Nest measures carefully, often keeping half the sachet back for the little one.

## 4. Day-in-the-life narrative

15:45, school pickup. 16:15, two hungry children and a homework table. Dinner is hours away and a snack fight is minutes away. Kettle on, a pan of water, one pack of Veggie Masala. Nest measures the tastemaker carefully: half for the little one who finds it too strong, the rest for the older one. Two plates, two forks, a small hand reaching up for the bowl. For two minutes nobody is asking for anything. Nest drinks a cup of tea standing up.

## 6. Why they choose Maggi

Maggi passes Nest's gate, **the child says yes**, more reliably than most alternatives. It is familiar across generations, so the parent trusts it from their own childhood; Maggi marked 50 years in India with a postal stamp (evidence, Medium–High). Nest also remembers that the brand came back after the 2015 India ban and November 2015 relaunch (evidence, High), which makes household trust something to keep earning, not something to assume.

Nest holds **MAGGI 2-Minute Noodles Veggie Masala**: familiar masala with visible vegetables on the pack, which reassures without the brand or the page making any nutrition claim. Nest buys single packs as frequent top-ups; the multipack is Stack's habit. Product names checked against the current range on maggi.in (2026-10-07).

## 12. Visual-world design system

Nest's world is a picture book on the kitchen table: pebble shapes, nest forms, round caps on every stroke, warm cream paper and a soft shadow under raised things. The constraint is safety made visible: **no sharp corners** (every radius at least 12 px) and **large touch targets** (at least 56 px), as if a child's hand might reach for anything.

**Accessibility notes.** Every text pair passes WCAG AA; tomato headings reach 4.84:1 on paper. Touch targets exceed WCAG's 44 px. Tomato and honey, the two sachet halves, stay clearly different under colour-blind simulation, and the half-portion mark also uses shape, not colour alone.

**Child safety.** No identifiable child faces and no child-directed claims anywhere in Nest's world. Children appear only as hands, silhouettes or toy-scale props (brief Section 22).

## 14. Recommendation likelihood and reasoning

**Steady: about 8 out of 10** (a design estimate, not survey data). Nest recommends inside trusted circles (family and parent groups) rather than publicly, and always as "a reliable backup" rather than a hero meal.

## 17. Risks, tensions and objections

- **Child-directed marketing.** Nothing in Nest's world may target children directly; check local rules on advertising to children before any use.
- **Health anxiety.** Parents feel judged for instant food. Nest's communication must not answer that with nutrition claims; anything near health is marked "needs Nestlé review".
- **Overlap with Stack.** Nest's problem is the child's acceptance at this meal; Stack's is planning the week. Nest buys top-ups; Stack buys multipacks.
- **Trust.** Household trust was tested in 2015 and has to keep being earned.

## 19. Motion-design direction

<!-- generated:motion:start -->
| Motion token | Value |
|---|---|
| Tempo feel (Section 20) | smooth |
| Idle loop | 4 s (96 frames at 24 fps) |
| Amplitude multiplier | 0.9 |
| Max squash/stretch | 6% |
| Anticipation | 6 frames |
| Overshoot | 4% |
| Stagger for secondary parts | 3 frames |
| Accent beats in the 4-beat bar | 1 |
| Pulse | 0 Hz |

Easing names are shared and fixed: `ease-in-soft`, `ease-out-pop`, `ease-settle`, `ease-linear-steam`.
<!-- generated:motion:end -->

Nest moves like a steady hand: smooth arcs, gentle settles, nothing sudden. Reaction loop: a warm nod and a small hop of relief. Micro-animation: the kettle pour. Consumption scene (8 s): school bag dropped, kettle on, measured sachet, a small hand reaching up, a quiet sip of tea.

**In 3D.** The idle, reaction and scene loops are built from rendered layers of the figure (head, body, arms, props, steam) and from an 8-view turntable, keeping these exact timings. Nothing is re-modelled for motion.

## 25. Custom "2" glyph, wordmark and numerals

Nest's "2" is one continuous yarn-noodle line with a small loop and round terminals, drawn at a 48-unit stroke so it reads at 16 px. Numerals share the rounded, generous counters, so times like "16:15" feel soft and friendly.
