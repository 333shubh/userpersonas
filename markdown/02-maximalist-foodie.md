## 1. Persona name and archetype

**The Maximalist Foodie**, embodied by **Riot**, a chaotic chili-noodle character with mismatched features, flame hair, stickers and bold patterns. The archetype is the home experimenter who treats a pack of noodles as a base layer: something to remix, top, sauce and film.

## 2. Short persona summary

Riot does not eat Maggi as it comes. The pack is a canvas for chili oil, cheese, a fried egg, crushed chips, whatever the internet dared this week. The meal is half food, half content: Riot's real question is "is this worth making and posting?". The joy is in the build, the reveal and the reaction from friends.

## 4. Day-in-the-life narrative

All day Riot collects ideas: a creator's chili-garlic oil hack at lunch, a street-food cart's topping on the way home. At 19:30 the kitchen becomes a set. Coloured lights on, phone propped against the spice jar. A pack of Special Masala, half the sachet in, the rest swapped for a squeeze of extra-hot sauce, a fried egg, crushed chips on top. It is filmed in three cuts before the noodles go soggy. The bite gets a reaction shot. The friends' group chat replies with fire emojis and "make me one".

## 6. Why they choose Maggi

Maggi passes Riot's gate, "is this worth making and posting?", because it is the most recognisable base: everyone knows the pack, so the remix reads instantly. It is cheap enough to fail with, and it holds up under sauces. Maggi's own Spicy campaign frames spice as Gen Z self-confidence (evidence, High), which is exactly Riot's attitude; Riot extends it rather than competing with it.

## 12. Visual-world design system

Riot's world looks like a sticker-bombed zine run through a risograph: thick ink keylines, die-cut stickers, starbursts and an ink plate printed 6 units off register. The clash is intentional and has a grammar:

1. Only four pairs may touch without an ink keyline: hot-pink × acid-green, volt-blue × tangerine, ultraviolet × yolk, chili × cyan.
2. Any other two colours that meet are separated by an ink keyline at the persona stroke width.
3. At most three clash colours, plus ink and paper, in any one region.
4. Text sits on ink, paper or a single flat colour, never across a clash seam.
5. Meaning is never carried by colour alone: stickers carry shape and a word.

**Accessibility notes.** Every text pair passes WCAG AA; chili is used for large display words only (4.17:1). Each clash pair was checked under protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia simulation and stays clearly different. Flame pulses run at 2 per second, under the 3-flash limit, and never as large high-contrast flicker.

## 17. Risks, tensions and objections

- **Random chaos.** The clash must stay grammatical; unruled colour turns into noise.
- **Brand dilution.** If the hack is the hero, Maggi becomes interchangeable. The pack must stay visible in every build.
- **Overlap with Mise.** Riot is cheap ingredients and loud results; Mise is curated and willing to pay. Riot's competitors are hacks and street food; Mise's are Korean and café noodles.
- **Spice and safety.** Spicy challenges must never encourage harm; heat is attitude, not a dare.

## 19. Motion-design direction

<!-- generated:motion:start -->
| Motion token | Value |
|---|---|
| Tempo feel (Section 20) | syncopated |
| Idle loop | 2 s (48 frames at 24 fps) |
| Amplitude multiplier | 1.4 |
| Max squash/stretch | 18% |
| Anticipation | 3 frames |
| Overshoot | 12% |
| Stagger for secondary parts | 1 frame |
| Accent beats in the 4-beat bar | 1.5, 3.5 |
| Pulse | 2 Hz (Flame pulse = one per beat (2 Hz), under the 3 per second ceiling. No large high-contrast flicker.) |

Easing names are shared and fixed: `ease-in-soft`, `ease-out-pop`, `ease-settle`, `ease-linear-steam`.
<!-- generated:motion:end -->

Riot never lands on the beat. Accents fall on the off-beats (the "and" after 1 and 3), with the biggest squash in the system and a 12% overshoot. Reaction loop: a full-body shout with flames spiking. Micro-animation: a sticker popping onto the pack. Consumption scene (8 s): hack spotted, sauce jet, egg drop, three-cut film, reaction bite.

**In 3D.** The idle, reaction and scene loops are built from rendered layers of the figure (head, body, arms, props, steam) and from an 8-view turntable, keeping these exact timings. Nothing is re-modelled for motion.

## 25. Custom "2" glyph, wordmark and numerals

Riot's "2" is a flame curl with a die-cut sticker outline and a 6-unit misregistered shadow. After QA the curl's taper was floored at 40 units (12.5% of cap height), so it reads at 16 px; the sticker outline counts toward the cap height. Numerals are heavy with mismatched widths and the same offset shadow, so "19:30" looks printed slightly wrong on purpose.
