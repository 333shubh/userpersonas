## 1. Persona name and archetype

**The Hostel Hungry**, embodied by **Cram**, a monochrome noodle-cup creature with headphones, a backpack and a fork. The archetype is the resourceful student or first-jobber who lives in shared housing without a real kitchen, and for whom a meal has one job: fill the gap, cheaply, with nothing left to wash.

## 2. Short persona summary

Cram eats Maggi because it is the cheapest complete meal that needs only a kettle, a cup and two minutes of attention. The pack is a tool, not a treat. Cram is not sad about it: there is pride in a system that works, a timer on the phone, the whole sachet in, and back to the notes. The emotional payoff is control, not comfort.

## 4. Day-in-the-life narrative

07:40, a missed breakfast. 09:00 to 12:30, lectures. 13:00 is the gap Cram plans around: the mess food is late or unloved, delivery is too expensive this week, and the next class is at 14:00. Kettle on, cup out, one 70 g pack of Masala, the whole tastemaker sachet in. Phone timer set. Notes open on the bed while it stands. Fork straight from the cup, cup rinsed in ten seconds, back to work by 13:15. The same pattern repeats at 23:00 during exams, when the hostel corridor smells of the same masala from three rooms.

## 6. Why they choose Maggi

Maggi passes Cram's gate, **price + zero cleanup**, better than anything else in reach. It is familiar, it is in every campus shop, and the flavour does not need extras to be finished. Cram never chose it as a brand statement; it is simply the default that never asks for a decision.

## 8. Preferred formats and variants

The default is the single 70 g pouch of **MAGGI 2-Minute Noodles Masala**. Cram buys singles, one or two at a time, never a multipack (that is Stack's job). Cup formats such as Masala Cuppa are a treat when the kettle is free but the cup is not. Product names checked against the current range on maggi.in (2026-10-07).

## 10. Illustration scene description

**13:00, hostel desk, flat fluorescent tube overhead.** Shadows fall straight down and hard. Cram sits slumped in a C-curve on a stack of notebooks; headphones on, noodle fringe over tired eyes, fork raised like a pen. The real Masala pack is clamped under one arm, redrawn in one ink as a photocopy-style halftone. A phone timer glows on the desk ("counts it on a phone timer"). A timetable is pasted to the wall at a 2° tilt with two staple marks. Density 2: at most five props, at least half the frame empty paper. Everything is black, white and halftone screens of the same ink.

## 12. Visual-world design system

Cram's world is a photocopied study note: one ink on paper, hard rectangles, ruled lines, a timetable grid and 1-bit halftone dots at 65 lpi. It is minimal, never boring: personality comes from type at full volume, crisp cut-paper edges and the confidence of using one colour well. Layout is a strict left-aligned column, like an exam answer sheet.

**Accessibility notes.** All text pairs meet **WCAG AAA** (7:1 body), the strictest in the system. Because the world has no hue at all, meaning never depends on colour; halftone density and shape do the separating work. Every image in Cram's world is shown in greyscale, including the real pack.

## 14. Recommendation likelihood and reasoning

**Likely, but quietly: about 7 out of 10** (a design estimate, not survey data). Cram recommends Maggi the way you recommend a phone charger: as the obvious tool, not with excitement. Word of mouth happens through shared packs and group chats, not posts.

## 17. Risks, tensions and objections

- **"Cheap and boring" collapse.** Cram must not read as sad. The black-and-white world has to feel sharp and witty, not poor.
- **Felt time vs real time.** Real cook time is longer than the "2-minute" name (evidence, Medium). Any copy that states cook times needs Nestlé review; Cram's line is about the felt two minutes, not a product claim.
- **Overlap with Lull.** Cram is economic and functional; Lull is emotional. Cram is never defined as "a student who eats late".
- **Fatigue.** The same taste, every day, is Cram's biggest switching risk.

Cram moves like someone running on four hours of sleep who is still faster than everyone else: a short anticipation, a sharp pop, then stillness. The reaction loop is the timer ringing, eyes snapping open. Micro-animation: the fork stabbing the noodles. Consumption scene (8 s): alarm, kettle click, whole sachet in, fork, back to the notes.

## 25. Custom "2" glyph, wordmark and numerals

Cram's "2" is cut from the cup cylinder: monoline, square terminals, a straight diagonal and a heavy base bar, drawn in the one ink. The fork-tine terminal was removed after QA because the tines fell below the 12.5% minimum stroke, and the glyph reads as a "2" at 16 px without them. Numerals follow the same monoline construction with timetable-mono widths, so times like "13:00" align in columns.
