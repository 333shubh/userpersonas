## 1. Persona name and archetype

**The Premium Flavor Explorer**, embodied by **Mise**, a sleek, long-limbed black-cat gentleman in a tailored oxblood coat, gold chopsticks in hand (a 3D designer vinyl figure). The archetype is the occasional trade-up cook who treats instant noodles as a starting point for a restaurant-style bowl, and is willing to pay for it.

## 2. Short persona summary

Mise does not eat Maggi in a hurry. Mise treats it as **mise en place**: everything prepared, plated and finished like a dish. The occasion is a choice, often for guests, and the question is "is it worth paying more?". Mise stays connected to Maggi's comfort: the premium bowl is still the same familiar warmth, just dressed for dinner.

## 4. Day-in-the-life narrative

A friend is coming over at 21:00. Mise has a Korean BBQ Chicken pack, a soft egg, spring onion and a finishing oil. Everything is chopped and laid out in small bowls before the water boils. The tastemaker is dissolved into a proper broth, the noodles are lifted high with chopsticks and laid in a ribbon, the egg is halved, and a thread of oil goes on last. Low pendant light, dark bowls, two glasses of water. The friend asks, "Is this really instant?". That is the point.

## 6. Why they choose Maggi

Maggi passes Mise's gate, "is it worth paying more?", when a premium variant delivers a real flavour step up while keeping the familiar comfort. Korean noodles are a fast-growing premium segment, and Maggi's Korean variants are priced far above its core packs (evidence, Medium). Mise's real competitors are Korean and café noodles, which are now easier than ever to get: Nongshim launched Shin Ramyun Kimchi Stir Fry in India in May 2026 through quick commerce (evidence, High).

## 12. Visual-world design system

Mise's world is a food-magazine spread: oxblood and cream in duotone, every in-between colour a tint of the two, and **exactly one foil element per layout**. Long 30° diagonals, generous negative space and editorial columns signal restraint. Restraint is the message: quality is shown by what is left out.

**Accessibility notes.** Every text pair passes WCAG AA. The foil reaches 6.24:1 on oxblood but only 2.03:1 on cream, so foil text only ever sits on the dark tone, and Mise layouts default to oxblood. The tastemaker and finishing-oil sachets stay clearly different under colour-blind simulation.

## 17. Risks, tensions and objections

- **Disconnect from comfort.** The brief forbids a premium persona that feels detached from Maggi's comfort-led identity; Mise's bowl must still feel warm.
- **Price justification.** If the flavour does not deliver, Mise switches to Korean and café competitors fast.
- **Cultural stereotype.** Global flavour comes from food and props only, never from costume or caricature; locale packs are reviewed locally.
- **Overlap with Riot.** Mise is curated and willing to pay; Riot is cheap ingredients and loud results.

Easing names are shared and fixed: `ease-in-soft`, `ease-out-pop`, `ease-settle`, `ease-linear-steam`.
<!-- generated:motion:end -->

Mise moves like a slow camera dolly: long, controlled arcs, no squash, no overshoot, every secondary part arriving four frames late. Reaction loop: a satisfied, poised nod. Micro-animation: chopsticks lifting a single noodle ribbon with a light sweep. Consumption scene (8 s): prep bowls, broth, ribbon lift, oil thread, plated bowl in pendant light.

**In 3D.** The idle, reaction and scene loops are built from rendered layers of the figure (head, body, arms, props, steam) and from an 8-view turntable, keeping these exact timings. Nothing is re-modelled for motion.

## 25. Custom "2" glyph, wordmark and numerals

Mise's "2" is a high-contrast didone with a chopstick-straight base stroke and a ball terminal. Its hairlines are held at the 12.5% minimum stroke so it still reads at 16 px, which brings the thick-to-thin contrast to about 2.6:1, a deliberate trade of drama for legibility. Numerals are old-style editorial figures.
