## 1. Persona name and archetype

**The Midnight Recharger**, embodied by **Lull**, a sleepy moon-faced noodle bowl whose steam becomes stars. The archetype is anyone landing after a long day or night: a shift worker home at midnight, a gamer after the last match, a professional after a late deadline. Per brief Section 17, Lull is as likely to be an adult, gamer or shift worker as a student, and is never "a student who eats late".

## 2. Short persona summary

Lull eats Maggi to come down, not to fuel up. The whole thing is a ritual: the slow tear of the sachet, the cup warming the hands, the steam in the dark. The question is emotional: "do I need comfort right now?". Where competitors sell energy, Lull is the **wind-down noodle** that helps you land. Lull stretches the two minutes on purpose.

## 4. Day-in-the-life narrative

The shift ends at 23:30. Home at 00:30, too wired to sleep, too tired to cook. Hoodie on, one lamp on, nothing else. A Masala Cuppa from the cupboard. The sachet is torn slowly, on purpose. Kettle water in, lid down, two hands around the warm cup. Steam rises in the lamp's glow while a quiet playlist runs. Eaten on the sofa, slowly. By 01:00 the day has finally ended, and Lull can sleep.

## 6. Why they choose Maggi

Maggi passes Lull's gate, "do I need comfort right now?", because it is warm, familiar and asks almost nothing. The cup format means no pan and no cleanup at midnight. The taste carries years of memories, so it comforts in a way a new snack cannot.

## 12. Visual-world design system

Lull's world is the room at night with one lamp on: deep blue-violet backgrounds, soft lavender text, a single warm glow and fine grain. The constraint is kindness to tired eyes: **no colour brighter than 75% relative luminance** (no pure white) and backgrounds no brighter than 3%. Shapes are crescents, soft orbs and slow S-curves; star points are kept small.

**Accessibility notes.** Every text pair passes WCAG AA; the warm glow on night reaches 10.84:1. The text face is Atkinson Hyperlegible, designed for readability. Bloom is limited to one light source per frame, and the glow pulse runs at a quarter of a beat per second, far below any flash threshold.

## 17. Risks, tensions and objections

- **Overlap with Cram.** Lull is emotional, not economic. Lull is never defined as a student who eats late.
- **Guilt.** Late eating can carry guilt; Lull's tone is comfort without judgement and without any claims.
- **Darkness and legibility.** Dark worlds can become hard to read; the luminance cap and Atkinson Hyperlegible keep text clear.
- **ASMR overuse.** ASMR cuts are reserved for Lull only, and always with captions and a silent version.

## 19. Motion-design direction

<!-- generated:motion:start -->
| Motion token | Value |
|---|---|
| Tempo feel (Section 20) | slow breathing |
| Idle loop | 4 s (96 frames at 24 fps) |
| Amplitude multiplier | 0.6 |
| Max squash/stretch | 0% |
| Anticipation | 12 frames |
| Overshoot | 0% |
| Stagger for secondary parts | 6 frames |
| Accent beats in the 4-beat bar | 1 |
| Pulse | 0.25 Hz (Glow breathes once per 4 s idle loop.) |

Easing names are shared and fixed: `ease-in-soft`, `ease-out-pop`, `ease-settle`, `ease-linear-steam`.
<!-- generated:motion:end -->

Lull moves on a slow breath: one inhale and exhale per 4-second loop, with no squash and no overshoot. Reaction loop: eyes softening, a contented sigh. Micro-animation: steam turning into stars. Consumption scene (8 s): door closes, lamp on, slow sachet tear, warm cup in both hands, steam rising into stars.

**In 3D.** The idle, reaction and scene loops are built from rendered layers of the figure (head, body, arms, props, steam) and from an 8-view turntable, keeping these exact timings. Nothing is re-modelled for motion.

## 25. Custom "2" glyph, wordmark and numerals

Lull's "2" is a filled crescent-moon arc with a steam tail, without a stroke, in soft lavender. Its widest parts swell well above the 12.5% minimum stroke, so it reads at 16 px even in the dark. Numerals are soft, round, low-contrast and wide.
